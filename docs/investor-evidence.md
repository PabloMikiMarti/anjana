# Anjana investor sections: evidence and claim boundaries

Checked 10 November 2026. Sections 5 (*Why now*) and 6 (*Beyond cardiovascular*) of `index.html` make the investment case. Every number on the page or in figures 7–8 is listed here with its source, how it was verified, and what it does **not** support.

## Section 5 · Why now

**Thesis, stated as a thesis on the page:** AI multiplies the molecules entering trials, and each one needs endpoints that sponsors and regulators trust. Committee adjudication is the gold standard, but its cost keeps many trials from using it. Anjana makes committee-grade review cheaper, so its market grows with the number of outcome trials, the events in each, and the share of trials that can afford adjudication. This matches the hero's positioning ("Make adjudication affordable, and it becomes the default").

## Section 6 · Beyond cardiovascular

**Product claim:** Reader 1 takes the endpoint definition as text, so a new endpoint starts as a new definition. That is already how the work moved from HF hospitalization (DELIVER) to CV death, MI and stroke (PARADISE-MI; site section 3.4). Intake, evidence quoting, uncertainty routing and sign-off are shared. The classifier is retrained per specialty, and each new endpoint needs validation. Pulmonary, renal and metabolic are labelled as **proposed** throughout.
