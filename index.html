1 · The problem

The gold standard is a committee of physicians, reading.

Slow

Closeout waits for the last dossier.

Costly

Physician time grows with every event.

Imperfectly reproducible

Two committees agree at only κ 0.60.

Hard to audit

The verdict is kept, the reasoning is not.

2 · How it works

Two independent readers, as in a committee.

Reader 1

Applies the endpoint definition

Checks each criterion and quotes the record.

Reader 2

Learned from physician committees

Predicts what the committee would decide.

When they agree, the decision stands with its evidence. When they disagree, a physician decides.

Every decision shows its work: five highlighted lines instead of forty pages.

A cockpit for the people who adjudicate.
